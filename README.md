# 📊 SaaS Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Insights

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to my data analysis project. 

In this project, I dove deep into IBM's subscription dataset to solve a real-world business problem: **Why are customers leaving, and how can we keep them?**

Using **Python, Pandas, Visualizations, and SQL**, I cleaned the raw data, conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and uncovered key behavioral patterns behind customer churn.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Data Source
* **Data Source:** IBM Telco / SaaS Customer Churn Dataset (Kaggle)
* **Dataset Size:** 7,000+ Customer Records
* **Objective:** Identify high-risk churn segments and recommend actionable retention strategies for subscription-based businesses.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights 

* **Contract Risk:** Customers on **Month-to-Month contracts** churn at a dramatically higher rate compared to those on 1-Year or 2-Year contracts. Encouraging annual sign-ups could immediately boost retention.
* **The First-Year Hurdle:** High churn happens mostly within the **first 12 months (low tenure)**, especially when paired with high monthly charges. Early onboarding experience is crucial.
* **Support Services Matter:** Customers who opted for **Tech Support and Online Security** stayed significantly longer than those who didn't.
* **Payment Friction:** Electronic check users showed higher churn rates compared to those on automated payment methods.

## 🎯 Next Steps & Recommendations for the Business
1. **Incentivize Long-Term Contracts:** Offer small discounts to transition Month-to-Month users into 1-Year plans.
2. **Improve Onboarding:** Focus retention efforts on new users in their first 90 days.
3. **Bundle Safety Features:** Push Tech Support as part of standard starter packages to increase stickiness.
